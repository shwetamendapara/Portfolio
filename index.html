Introduction

About Me

I am a dedicated and detail-oriented pharmacy postgraduate student currently pursuing M.Pharm (Pharmaceutics) at K.B. Institute of Pharmaceutical Education and Research, Kadi Sarva Vishwavidyalaya, Gandhinagar.

My expertise lies in Regulatory Affairs, strengthened through active participation in a comprehensive Regulatory Affairs conference covering global regulatory frameworks, CTD/ECTD submissions, ICH guidelines, EU–US regulatory pathways, quality compliance, pharmacovigilance, and lifecycle management.

I have hands-on industrial experience through a Project Internship at Amneal Pharmaceuticals (PHARMEZ) in Formulation & Development of Oral Solid Dosage forms, where I was exposed to granulation, compression, coating, and regulatory documentation under senior scientists.

My academic research focuses on advanced drug delivery systems — including solid dispersions for bioavailability enhancement, mesoporous nanoparticles for targeted delivery, and gastro-retentive floating tablet formulations.

Research Projects

Development & Evaluation of Everolimus Solid Dispersion Tablets
M.Pharm Project I · 2024–2026
Enhanced Solubility and Dissolution · KSV, Gandhinagar

Focused on improving the oral bioavailability of Everolimus — a BCS Class IV drug — using solid dispersion techniques. The project aims to overcome solubility limitations of the drug through carrier-based solid dispersion systems for enhanced dissolution performance.

Solid dispersion formulation for Everolimus (BCS Class IV)
Comprehensive evaluation of solubility profiles
Dissolution rate comparison vs conventional formulations
Bioavailability enhancement using carrier polymers
Development of Mesoporous Silica Nanoparticles
M.Pharm Project II · 2024–2026
Targeted Drug Delivery · KSV, Gandhinagar

Investigating the synthesis and comprehensive characterization of mesoporous silica nanoparticles for targeted drug delivery applications. The study explores surface modification strategies to enhance drug loading efficiency and achieve controlled/sustained release profiles.

Synthesis & characterization of mesoporous silica nanoparticles
Surface modification techniques for targeted delivery
Enhanced drug loading efficiency studies
Controlled release profile optimization
Formulation of Herbal Effervescent Floating Tablet
B.Pharm Final Year Project · 2023–2024
Gastro-retentive Drug Delivery · Saurashtra University, Rajkot

Successfully formulated a gastro-retentive floating tablet utilizing Aegle marmelos (Bael) fruit extract. The project evaluated effervescence characteristics, buoyancy duration, and sustained release behavior for improved gastric residence time and therapeutic efficacy.

Gastro-retentive floating tablet with Aegle marmelos extract
Effervescence time and buoyancy lag evaluation
Sustained release profile assessment
Herbal-pharmaceutical formulation development
